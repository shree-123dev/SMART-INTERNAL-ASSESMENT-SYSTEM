About SIAMS Project

Smart Internal Assessment & Management System

SIAMS is a web-based educational ERP developed to automate Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE), student attendance, practical marks, and automated grade reporting for higher education institutions.

🎯 Problem Statement & Institutional Vision

Traditional manual internal assessment processes rely heavily on paper records and disconnected spreadsheets. This workflow causes substantial administrative delays, human calculation errors, loss of continuous evaluation records, and delayed feedback to students.

SIAMS solves this problem by providing:

Real-Time Evaluation

Instant recording of marks during laboratory practicals and tutorials.

Contactless QR Attendance

Accurate attendance logging with encrypted student QR identities.

Performance Analytics

Comprehensive reports on student academic progress and subject averages.

100% Eco-Friendly & Paperless

Zero paper waste with instant cloud-ready digital records.
